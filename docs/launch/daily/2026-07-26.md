## Decisions

- Use a strongly relational PostgreSQL core with stable logical content identities and immutable
  version rows. JSONB is limited to provider payload mirrors, extensible immutable evidence, and
  Audit metadata rather than authoritative business structure.
- A Published Section or Lesson with purchase/progress history may be retired from the current
  Course structure, but it remains available to the existing Students whose Entitlements cover it;
  its commercial and learning history is preserved.
- Replace the fixed 150-day Entitlement duration with an Admin-managed Course access end date,
  normally aligned to semester end. Section purchases inherit the containing Course's date, and
  checkout snapshots/discloses the exact expiry before payment.
- An Admin may extend or shorten an individual Entitlement with a required reason and immutable
  Audit evidence. Moving its expiry into the past ends access immediately without deleting
  Enrollment or Progress. The exact notification and API behavior will be frozen on July 27.
- `access_ends_at` is an exact UTC `timestamptz`; access is permitted only while
  `current_timestamp < entitlement.access_ends_at`. A Kuwait-local calendar date is stored as the
  exclusive first instant of the following local day converted to UTC.
- Sections have no independent expiry override. `order_item.access_ends_at` is the immutable
  commercial snapshot; `entitlement.original_access_ends_at` preserves it, while
  `entitlement.access_ends_at` is authoritative after Admin adjustments.
- Admin expiry adjustments require elevated authorization and immutably record old/new expiry,
  reason, actor, timestamp, and any applicable support/refund reference.
- Retirement eligibility uses the Order's eligible commercial-terms acceptance or zero-value
  free-grant timestamp, not delayed webhook/row-insertion time. Retirement blocks future
  acquisition without invalidating earlier otherwise-active Entitlements.
- Optimistic writes use compare-and-swap on aggregate revision; idempotency is scoped by actor or
  merchant, command, and key; request-fingerprint mismatch is rejected; outbox events use stable
  event IDs.
- A Course has at most one mutable `DRAFT` revision. Submission freezes its complete graph;
  reviewed/approved revisions are immutable, and a change request clones a new draft.
- Staff invitation creation does not create an Account. Acceptance locks and consumes the
  invitation/token, then atomically creates the Account, credential, acceptance evidence, Audit,
  and outbox records.
- The current approved Course pointer uses a same-Course composite foreign key; historical approval
  evidence remains immutable after supersession.
- Taxonomy kind is relationally enforced. Major and Subject remain independent MVP dimensions
  because no compatibility rule is approved.
- Retirement is an explicit elevated command, never inferred from omission. Checkout is blocked
  while the applicable current approved graph contains retired content.
- Media uses type-specific post-scan branches, immutable attempts, terminal source rejection,
  exact successful-attempt references, immutable object identity, and a real cryptographic/provider
  checksum rather than multipart ETag.
- Every Account has exactly one role assigned at creation and immutable during MVP. Only Students
  purchase, receive ordinary Entitlements, create Enrollments, or record Progress. Instructor/Admin
  Accounts have no Student learning capability; Admin preview remains separate and audited.
- Every ordinary Entitlement originates from exactly one paid or zero-value Coupon Order. Admins may
  adjust existing expiry but cannot create access through a separate manual-grant command.
- Paid Coupon Orders reserve exact capacity at Order acceptance, consume it on verified timely
  capture, and release unused reservations on cancellation/expiry. Zero-value Orders consume
  immediately; full Refund releases Student eligibility without restoring historical global quota.
- Orders explicitly preserve acceptance, deadline, completion, cancellation, expiry, and
  reconciliation state. Attempt uniqueness covers `CREATED/PENDING/UNKNOWN`; immutable provider
  events/state history preserve every observation.
- Entitlements separately store `acquired_at` and `retirement_eligibility_at = order.accepted_at`.
  A grandfathered paid Order must also have verified capture within its payment deadline.
- The Student/Course access guard is locked at Order acceptance and paid completion. A conflicting
  second successful payment enters reconciliation and cannot create duplicate access.
- An active Section Entitlement may coexist with a later Course Entitlement; there is no credit,
  proration, expiry combination, or cross-revocation. The inverse duplicate purchases remain blocked.
- Catalog delisting blocks discovery/new checkout but preserves qualifying existing access.
  Retirement remains a future-acquisition/inclusion block; only constrained elevated emergency
  Course access suspension immediately blocks existing runtime access without changing Entitlements.
- Full Refund revokes only the source Order's Entitlement. There is no generic Admin revoke;
  zero-value reversal requires a defined reconciliation/policy/legal workflow and evidence.
- Progress writes require runtime Lesson access and qualifying graph reachability. Completion is
  server-derived from the exact played Asset Version's trusted duration and retains the completing
  version as permanent evidence.
- Each paid Order's earning entry snapshots the Course owner and effective revenue-share version.
  Ownership changes affect later Orders only; Refund/chargeback adjustments stay with the original
  earning and Instructor.
- Office Hours uses a time-derived `UPCOMING/LIVE/ENDED` phase; ending proves no delivery or
  attendance. Joining is live-window-only, while cancellation preserves all history.
- Moderation reports preserve both the stable logical target and exact visible version. Automated
  findings never silently hide/retire content or replace separate Media/security safety workflows.
- Notification Events relationally snapshot exact Account/channel Recipients at source-event time;
  mandatory notices cannot be suppressed and email failure cannot invalidate the in-app record.
- Every financial change is an immutable source-linked ledger entry. Statement approval freezes
  items/totals; partial payment is prohibited, negative balances carry, and only affected
  Statements are blocked by reconciliation.
- PostgreSQL contains the durable work, lease, attempt, error, and completion state needed to
  rebuild Redis queues. Ambiguous external effects reconcile before retry.
- Remote retention deletion is exact-version, serialized with legal holds, externally verified,
  and confirmed in a second PostgreSQL transaction; pending/failure never appears completed.
