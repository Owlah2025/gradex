### The CI failure, and what it exposed

Migration `0006_authenticated_sessions` raised the schema to version 6 and
`backend/internal/db/schema.go` raised `MaxSchemaVersion` to match, but the Migrations job still
asserted version 5 behind a comment reading "Keep in step with MaxSchemaVersion in
internal/db/schema.go". The expected-object list needed no change: `0006` only widens the
`identity_security_events_type` CHECK constraint to admit the five session event types and creates
no relations. `7d8710e` corrects the assertion.

Two facts are worth recording rather than filing as a fixed one-liner:

1. **The full local gate suite was green while the range was red on CI.** No local script asserts
   schema version — the check exists only in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. "All local gates pass" was
   a true statement that carried no information about this failure.
2. **An independent reviewer approved a range that did not build.** `agy` returned 0/0/0/0 on
   `ad1b8f6..e21d0e4` while CI was failing on that exact head. This is correct behaviour, not a
   reviewer defect: the nine review dimensions cover the diff, not gate execution. It does mean a
   verdict alone is not evidence that a range passes, and the acceptance contract is right to
   require local gates, hosted CI, **and** review as separate items.

Both are carried into S1B3 as `CARRYOVER-S1B2-CI-DRIFT` below.
