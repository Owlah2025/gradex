## Acceptance Evidence

S1C — and therefore S1 — closes only when:

1. an Admin can invite an Instructor and an Admin, and only an Admin can;
2. the invitee sets their own initial password, the inviting Admin never learns it, and the plaintext
   never reaches storage, logs, argv, or telemetry;
3. an invitation secret is digest-only, expiring, purpose-bound, and consumed exactly once under real
   PostgreSQL contention, and cannot grant a role other than the one invited;
4. a suspended account's **already-issued** session is refused on its next request, proven through the
   HTTP boundary and mutation-checked against a vacuous pass;
5. the full role and ownership authorization matrix denies every wrong-role and wrong-owner direct API
   call across every mounted protected route, and a new route cannot be added without a matrix row;
6. bootstrap test 3 is rerun across the complete protected Identity and staff surface and recorded as
   the **final** full-surface proof;
7. the shorter Admin financial/security recent-authentication window is proven before those
   capabilities are reachable;
8. Arabic/English staff screens pass responsive, accessibility, lint, typecheck, and a **clean**
   production build, and persist no secret in the browser;
9. all three inherited policy calls (B1–B3) carry a recorded `CONFIRM` or `OVERTURN` with a check;
10. both gate-fidelity carryovers (C1, C2) are closed by gates that are negative-tested to fail;
11. full local gates, hosted CI, and independent review all pass with no critical or high finding.

Item 11 lists three separate requirements deliberately, and the reason is recorded evidence rather
than caution: S1B2 returned a clean independent verdict on a range that did not build, and had a green
local suite while hosted CI was red. They are not interchangeable.
