How a Gradex course works

Four steps, in order. Each one builds on the last — watching is where you start, not where you stop.

  1. 01

    Watch the lecture

    Clear, exam-focused videos. Resume from where you left off, on any device.

  2. 02

    Study the notes

    Download slides and notes that get to the point — made to revise from, not to pad the runtime.

  3. 03

    Build the lab

    Download the project files and guide, and build something real with what you just learned.

  4. 04

    Ask in the community

    Stuck on the lab? Ask in the course community and keep the follow-up going until it clicks.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I actually get when I buy a course?
A Course purchase covers all of its Sections; a Section purchase covers that Section only. The exact semester-aligned access expiry is shown before checkout and includes the protected videos plus any Resources and Lab Materials available in the purchased scope.
How do I pay?
Course and Section prices are shown in KWD. Payment is completed on Tap's hosted checkout, so Gradex does not collect or store your card details.
Are the courses in Arabic or English?
The Gradex interface is available in Arabic and English. Each Course keeps the language chosen by its Instructor, which is shown on the Course details; Gradex does not automatically translate Course content.
What happens after I finish a course?
Your Course or Section access remains active until the exact expiry disclosed at checkout, unless an authorized support adjustment changes it. Your learning progress remains recorded after access expires, and you can purchase access again through the normal checkout.
Can I watch on my phone?
Yes. Video adapts to your connection and resumes from your last position across phone, tablet, and laptop.
